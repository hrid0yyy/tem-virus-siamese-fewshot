Highlights

- Rare viruses in negative-stain TEM recognised from few examples, without retraining
- First few-shot evaluation of all 22 viruses of the public TEM dataset, official split
- With one reference particle per rare virus, Siamese features beat cross-entropy ones
- Errors fall mainly between related or look-alike viruses, e.g. the arenaviruses
- A linear classifier on frozen features raises rare-virus F1 without retraining
