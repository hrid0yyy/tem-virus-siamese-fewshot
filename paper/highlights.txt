Highlights

- Few-shot benchmark of all 22 viruses of the public TEM dataset on its official split
- Siamese contrastive encoder matches a strong re-trained classifier on 14 base viruses
- With one example per rare virus, Siamese features beat cross-entropy features
- Rare viruses are linearly separable; nearest-prototype rules cause most errors
- A validation-selected linear classifier on frozen features lifts rare-virus F1
